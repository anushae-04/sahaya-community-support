Before you help

Help safely

  • Prescription medicines must come from a licensed pharmacy against a valid prescription. Never hand them over without one.
  • Meet carefully. Prefer a public place or a building gate, tell someone where you are going, and do not enter a home alone if you feel unsure.
  • Respect privacy. Collect only what you need, do not share a person's details in public groups, and never accept money or documents to hold.
