About · Sahaya

A helping hand for every neighbourhood.

Sahaya is a community support platform. It helps people ask for practical help when they need it, and gives neighbours a simple way to offer it.

Our purpose

Every neighbourhood has people who need help and people who can help. Sahaya exists to connect them clearly, safely and kindly.

Needs like medicine, food, a ride to the hospital or a safe place to stay are often solved by someone nearby. The hard part is knowing who to ask and how to ask.

Sahaya makes that first step easier. You say what you need, we help you put it into a clear request, and you share it with people you trust.

What we believe
01

Dignity first

Asking for help should never feel like a burden. Every request is treated with respect, whatever the need.

02

Privacy by design

The request pages run in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to Sahaya. You decide what to share and with whom.

03

Safety together

Every page carries plain safety tips for both people asking and people helping, and points to emergency numbers.

04

Local and human

Help works best between people who live near each other. Sahaya supports that, rather than replacing it.

03

Questions
Is Sahaya an emergency service?

No. If someone is in danger or needs urgent medical care, call 112, or 108 for an ambulance, straight away. Sahaya is for everyday practical needs and supports emergency services without replacing them.

Does Sahaya store what I type?

No. The request pages only prepare text in your browser so you can copy it or share it yourself. Nothing is sent to Sahaya. Avoid adding details you would not want the person receiving it to see.

Can I ask for help on behalf of someone else?

Yes. You can fill in a request for a parent, neighbour or friend. Use their area and the contact details they are happy to share.

Who can volunteer?

Anyone who wants to help. Choose the ways you are comfortable helping, share a short profile with a group you trust, and follow the safety tips on the volunteer page.
